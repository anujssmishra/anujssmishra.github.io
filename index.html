    Research

    My work asks how distributed ledgers and digital public infrastructure change cyber risk, and how organizations and regulators can manage it.

    I combine analytical risk models and simulation with text mining and interviews with practitioners and experts.

    • Cyber risk in blockchain systems

      My dissertation develops strategic risk models for blockchain-enabled organizations: how vulnerabilities are assessed and mitigated, and when the technology delivers real security benefits.

      Related work: InCIS 2026

    • Central bank digital currencies and payments

      Utility-based cyber-risk models and simulations that compare CBDCs with UPI and card payments, and qualitative studies of how emerging economies introduce a digital currency.

      Related work: AMCIS 2025, MDC 2025, ETGovernment

    • Self-sovereign identity

      How decentralized identity scales in public services, studied through DigiYatra, the credential system used at Indian airports.

      Related work: AMCIS 2026

    • Telecom spam and regulatory enforcement

      Distributed-ledger enforcement of consumer preferences against unsolicited commercial calls and messages, and systems-theoretic (STAMP) analysis of compliance across networks.

      Related work: ICIS 2026, JAIS workshop at ECIS 2026

    • AI on agricultural platforms

      How Indian AgTech platforms use AI to win and keep farmers, studied through case studies, news analysis and interviews.

      Related work: Pre-ICIS SIGDSA 2026
